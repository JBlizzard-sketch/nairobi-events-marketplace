Event vendor marketplace


You are building a full production-grade event vendor discovery and quoting marketplace for the Nairobi corporate and social events market. Read the full brief, expand the vision, present ~20 substantial phases, wait for "next."
The pain:
Planning a corporate event, product launch, wedding, or private party in Nairobi means making 15 separate WhatsApp conversations with strangers who may or may not be legitimate, who quote inconsistently, who ghost you, and who you have no basis to trust. There is no single place where you describe your event and get structured, comparable quotes from vetted vendors — caterers, MCs, florists, photographers, AV technicians, tent and furniture suppliers, event security. Every procurement manager at a Nairobi corporate has felt this pain. Every couple planning a wedding has felt it. The market for event services in Kenya is enormous and completely unstructured.
What you are building:
A platform where an event planner (corporate or individual) fills in a structured event brief — event type, date, location, guest count, budget range, services needed — and within 4 hours receives 3 competing quotes from vetted vendors in each category they need. Vendors are vetted on registration (portfolio, references, business registration), and rated by past clients. The platform takes a commission on confirmed bookings and charges vendors a monthly listing fee for premium visibility. Corporate clients get a dedicated dashboard for managing multiple events, budgets, and vendor relationships.
Critical nuances:
The quote comparison interface is the core of the product — it must make it genuinely easy to compare vendors side by side on price, inclusions, and rating, not just a list of emails. Vendor vetting must be real — not just "sign up and you're listed." A poorly performing vendor (bad ratings, no-shows) should be suspended automatically. Payment should flow through the platform — not directly to vendors — so the platform can enforce escrow (vendor paid after event completion) which dramatically reduces fraud. Corporate procurement teams need invoicing and PO compatibility. The brief submission flow must be fast — under 5 minutes — or people will abandon and go back to WhatsApp.
Expand this. Consider: a vendor "availability calendar" so you can instantly see who's free on your date; a planning timeline tool that tells you when to book which vendor type; a post-event vendor report card sent automatically; a "emergency vendor" feature for last-minute needs; a white-label version for large event companies managing their supplier network; and AI-powered budget optimisation that suggests vendor combinations that fit your brief and budget best.
Hosting: Next.js on Vercel. Backend API and database on Railway. Background jobs for quote request routing and notification. Docker-ready for scale.
